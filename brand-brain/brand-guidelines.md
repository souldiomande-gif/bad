# Brand guidelines — oraimo (as sold on doya.ai)

Sources: oraimo about pages (be.oraimo.com/pages/about-us,
ng.oraimo.com), oraimo Kenya/Nigeria social campaigns, product listings.

## Voice and tone

oraimo speaks like a confident tech brand for ambitious everyday people — not
a luxury brand, not a bargain bin. Energetic, benefit-first, lightly playful.

**Do:**
- Lead with the felt benefit, back with the number: "Le silence total. 52 dB
  de réduction de bruit."
- Use the exploration/space metaphor the Space Series is built on: "Ton espace
  s'ouvre." / "Noise off, space on."
- Playful confidence toward competitors (real oraimo ad: "We're not saying
  yours are bad, but…").
- Short sentences. Emoji in social captions (🔋 🌍 ✨) — oraimo's own accounts
  do this.

**Don't:**
- Luxury-speak ("exquisite", "premium craftsmanship") — it's aspirational but
  accessible.
- Spec dumps without a benefit attached.
- Overclaim the AI translation (it supports major world languages; a Nigerian
  reviewer noted it lacks Yoruba/Hausa/Igbo — don't promise local languages
  it doesn't have).

## Vocabulary

Uses: ANC hybride, réduction de bruit, autonomie, IA / AI Smart, son spatial,
Gris spatial, "Keep Exploring", Master the Chaos.
Avoids: "cheap"/"pas cher" as identity (say "au juste prix", value framing).

## Visual identity (observed)

- Product: matte space-grey buds + pebble-shaped case with light ring
  ("Infinite Light Effect") — the glowing case is the most photogenic,
  ownable visual; feature it.
- oraimo brand green as accent; dark/space backgrounds for the Space Series
  (stars, cosmic gradients), clean white for marketplace listings.
- Photography style in oraimo campaigns: young urban African adults —
  commuting, gym, open-plan office, campus — real-life chaos the ANC removes.
